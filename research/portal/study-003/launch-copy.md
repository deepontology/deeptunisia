# Citizen Budget 2027: Draft Launch Copy

## 1. Study Page Text

This study invites citizens to balance Tunisia's 2027 state budget. In about five minutes, you adjust spending across major public sectors to reflect your priorities, working within a realistic balanced total. 91 dinars in every 100 the state raises itself come from tax. Understanding how public funds are gathered and allocated helps ground our collective conversations about public services, subsidies, and national investments.

All figures presented in this draft instrument are currently being checked against official finance ministry documents and parliamentary mission tables. The study will not open until every category total is verified against the final adopted law. Once the final budget law is adopted by parliament, the interactive tool will be made available. Comparing different allocations helps clarify the trade-offs involved in public finance.

Participation is completely anonymous and voluntary. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, or browser fingerprints. We record only your chosen category allocations, two brief follow-up questions, an optional broad region, and an anonymous receipt code for your records.

This project is run by DeepTunisia as an open research exercise. Because participants are self-selected, results will describe only the people who took part and cannot represent the entire country.

## 3. What This Study Cannot Say

### English
This study is based on an open, self-selected sample. It cannot describe national consensus or general public opinion. It cannot determine what public spending should be, nor can it evaluate whether any specific budget line is wasteful. The findings reflect solely the individual choices of the respondents who took part.
