{#intro}

Every row here is a position record with no recorded end. They are **not** equally current, so they are not shown together: an office confirmed last week and one last confirmed two years ago are different claims about the present, and merging them would make this page most confident exactly where it is weakest.

{#silent}

These posts have holders in the record but none left open, most recently held on the date shown. Some are genuinely abolished, some are vacant, and some are simply gaps in this map — the dataset does not currently distinguish between those three, which is itself worth knowing.

{#foot}

The comparison against an office's median tenure is descriptive, not predictive. An office being past its median forecasts nothing about whether its holder is about to change; it says only that this office has historically changed hands more often than this. Medians are shown only where at least four holders are recorded. The turnover figures behind them are on the [Atlas](/atlas), and the offices whose chronology is unresolved are listed on the [evidence page](/evidence).
