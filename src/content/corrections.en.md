Every commit that touched the dataset, newest first. This page is generated from version control rather than written, so nobody decides which changes are flattering enough to appear. A claim that was wrong and later fixed leaves a permanent, dated trace here.

{#foot}

Line counts are a proxy for scale, not for significance: a one-line change that corrects a date matters more than a hundred lines of new citations. Read the commit for what actually happened.
