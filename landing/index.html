The rule

An inference is never allowed to become a fact.

  • Documented An official record, decree or gazette entry states it. 199 claims · solid
  • Reported Credible publications report it, with attribution. 691 claims · dashed
  • Inferred Nobody states it. Reasoned from documented structure — and it must publish what would prove it wrong. 39 claims · dotted
  • Unsubstantiated It circulates without reliable evidence. Recorded, attributed, and off by default. 12 claims · dot-dash

Why this exists

Read the argument

Deep Tunisia exists so that Tunisia remembers: seventy years of appointments, institutions, ruptures and relationships, held in one place, in the open, in a form anyone can audit.

The website is a window. The knowledge graph is the project.

No language model writes claims into this graph. Knowledge enters through one door, in a fixed order, and the build refuses anything that arrives out of order. Models draft translations and research candidates — flagged and gated. See how this was made.

A record that publishes only its strengths is advertising.

Everyone in this graph is held to the same rules.

Deep Tunisia belongs to no party, no government and no ideology. It is open source under version control, its exports are downloadable in full, and its methodology, corrections history and funding are published on the site rather than described in a brochure.

A discussion layer is designed, anchored to each record. It is closed; its posting budget and hold are specified, not wired.

No name. No email. No password. Nothing to seize.
